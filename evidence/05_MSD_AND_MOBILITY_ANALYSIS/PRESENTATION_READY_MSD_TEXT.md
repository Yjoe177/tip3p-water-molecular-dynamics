# Molecular Mobility: Mean Squared Displacement

## Main explanation

MSD measures how far water molecules move from their previous positions as lag time increases. The oxygen MSD is consistently higher at the 300 K target than at the 273 K target, showing higher short-time molecular mobility at the warmer temperature.

## Results

At 1.0 ps: 3.414 Å² (300 K) vs 2.380 Å² (273 K).

At 2.0 ps: 6.436 Å² (300 K) vs 4.345 Å² (273 K).

## Interpretation

The cooler water is less mobile, consistent with its more populated hydrogen-bond network and greater tetrahedral ordering.

## Limitation

The trajectories are only ~5 ps. No common fitting window satisfied all criteria for a reliable diffusion coefficient, so no converged bulk D is claimed.
