# Limitations

- Trajectories are short and adjacent frames are correlated.
- Lineages are related; there are no independent temperature replicates, p-values, CIs, or causal temperature-effect claim.
- Pressure mean is not equilibration proof.
- The 273 K restart has one genuine missing thermo record; RNG continuity is not claimed.
- No diffusion, long-time convergence, phase-transition, ice, or rigorous-equilibrium result is claimed.
