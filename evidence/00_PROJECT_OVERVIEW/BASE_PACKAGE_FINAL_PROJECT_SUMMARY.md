# Final TIP3P Water Project Summary

Experiment 1 is the original 300 K R7R simulation, distinct from the matched-study T1 300 K reference. It contains 862 TIP3P waters (2586 atoms), 1 fs steps, PME, 8 A cutoff, runtime SETTLE, Middle Langevin, accepted 80 ps Bussi-NPT equilibration, and accepted segmented 6 ps + 4 ps Bussi-NPT production. The primary 107.720–112.620 ps window (n=50) gives T 300.3862 ± 5.6816 K, P 9.369 ± 483.600 bar, density 0.974238 ± 0.000979 g/cm3, and potential -8203.5226 ± 34.1046 kcal/mol. Exact volume is 26468.729 ± 32.497 A3 (n=9). Rigid water gives O-H 0.95719658 ± 0.00004713 A, H-H 1.51359458 ± 0.00007450 A, and H-O-H 104.490602 ± 0.000091 degrees. The illustrative 14-frame intermolecular RDF has OO 2.75 A (g=2.60726; min 3.45 A), OH 1.85 A (g=1.24201; min 2.45 A), and HH 2.45 A (g=1.24726; min 2.95 A); large-r means are approximately one.

Experiment 2 is a matched 300 K-target versus 273 K-target comparison. Sampled temperatures are 301.7867 ± 5.3031 K (n=100) and 269.9610 ± 4.7604 K (n=99); density 0.976319 ± 0.001366 versus 0.976066 ± 0.000786 g/cm3; potential -8214.3944 ± 36.2132 versus -8518.8839 ± 27.1919 kcal/mol; and exact volume 26409.265 ± 36.923 versus 26415.944 ± 21.317 A3. Pressure is -75.25 ± 395.94 versus -592.70 ± 357.78 bar: instantaneous NPT pressure is extremely noisy and its mean is not evidence of equilibration. H-bond events/frame are 1108.70 ± 17.00 versus 1195.86 ± 17.19; per-water values 2.5724 ± 0.0394 versus 2.7746 ± 0.0399. Accepted geometry is 2.8614 ± 0.1910 versus 2.8523 ± 0.1809 A and 162.991 ± 7.311 versus 163.393 ± 7.277 degrees. q is 0.5617 ± 0.1947 versus 0.5908 ± 0.1922; common-radius 3.5-A coordination is 5.2066 versus 5.1170. OO first peak is 2.775 A, sharper at 273 K; robust OH features occur at 1.825 and 3.225 A; The first H–H feature shifts from 2.475 Å at the 300 K target to 2.425 Å at the 273 K target; the principal later robust H–H feature is at 3.775 Å in both branches. The cooler-target branch is geometrically more H-bonded and more tetrahedrally ordered, not direct evidence of stronger H-bond energy.

Limitations: short correlated trajectories, related lineages, no independent replicates/p-values/CIs/causal claim, pressure not equilibration proof, one genuine 273-K restart-boundary missing thermo record, no RNG-continuity claim, and no diffusion, long-time convergence, phase-transition, ice, or rigorous-equilibrium claim.
